# Case 05 — The gym sent debt collectors after me

**Debt Consumer Case 05** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Messy, Mild

**Claimant:** Danielle R. Brooks, 30, North London

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

I joined a gym chain about 14 months ago. I thought it was a 12-month contract. When I hit the 12-month mark I emailed their general enquiries address to cancel. It took them two weeks to reply and they told me I'd actually auto-renewed for another 12 months and that I needed to send written notice to a different email address.

I sent another email to the address they gave me saying I wanted to cancel. No reply. I then sent a recorded delivery letter to their registered office. Still no reply. After a month of silence I just cancelled the direct debit through my bank, I didn't know what else to do.

Now they've sent me a letter from a debt collection agency saying I owe £450 for the remaining 10 months of the 'renewed' contract. I'm really stressed about this. I don't think it's fair that they can just auto-renew my contract when I tried to cancel, especially when I couldn't even get anyone to respond to me.

Can they actually do this? I've got the original contract but the auto-renewal bit is buried in the small print.
