# Case 07 — The council keeps fobbing me off about the damp

**Housing Disrepair Case 07** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Messy, Moderate

**Claimant:** Crystal Bello, 38, single mother, South London

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

I've been dealing with damp and black mould in my council flat for about eight months now. It's mainly in my children's bedroom and the bathroom but it's started spreading into the hallway too.

I first reported it by phone. The council sent someone round after about three weeks, he said it was a 'condensation issue' and told me to open windows more and stop drying clothes indoors. I pushed back because I know it's not condensation, the walls are wet to the touch when it rains.

They eventually sent a contractor who put in a dehumidifier and fitted a new extractor fan in the bathroom. They asked me to sign a form when the work was done. I signed it, I thought I was just confirming they'd been, but looking back at it now I'm worried it might say I agreed the problem was fixed. The wording is a bit ambiguous.

The mould came back within a month, worse than before. My youngest has developed a persistent cough and our GP has written a letter saying the living conditions may be contributing to it.

I've sent about 15 emails to the council since this started, with photos, chasing for updates. Their replies have been patchy, mostly just acknowledging my emails and saying they'll investigate further. Two months ago I was assigned a new case handler who hasn't contacted me once.

I don't know what I'm supposed to do next. I haven't made a formal complaint or sent any kind of legal letter. A friend mentioned something about a 'pre-action protocol' but I don't know what that means. I just want my children to live somewhere safe.
