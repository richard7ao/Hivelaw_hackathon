# Case 08 — The builder left the job half done and now he's threatening to sue me

**Contract Building Case 08** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Messy, Moderate

**Claimant:** Neil Whitlock, 52, Leeds

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

I hired a local contractor to renovate my kitchen, the agreed price was £12,000. There's no proper contract, we agreed it over text and a conversation in my kitchen. He sent me a message saying '12k for the full job as discussed' and I replied 'sounds good, when can you start'.

He started well enough, they stripped out the old kitchen, did the rough plumbing and electrics, started fitting the new units. Then he just stopped turning up. For about a month he kept saying he'd be back 'next week' and never was.

Then he started saying there was extra work that I'd need to pay for, specifically moving a gas pipe and upgrading the electrical board which would be another £3,500 on top. I never agreed to any extras. He says I did verbally but I absolutely didn't.

After weeks of this I lost patience and withheld the last £4,000 of the £12,000. I'd already paid him £8,000 in two bank transfers. He went ballistic and sent me a text saying I was in breach and I'd be 'hearing from his solicitor'.

My family needed a functioning kitchen so I paid another contractor £800 to do the most urgent bits, they reconnected the sink and put in temporary worktops. I know this might have complicated things but we couldn't carry on without a kitchen.

I've since got a quote from a different contractor saying it'd cost £6,500 to finish everything to the original spec.

I'm also worried because the electrical work the first contractor did was never signed off by Building Control. Is that a problem?
