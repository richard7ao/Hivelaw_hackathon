# Case 02 — Our wedding photographer didn't show up

**Contract Consumer Case 02** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Fresh

**Claimants:** Sarah and Tom Mitchell, Early 30s, Bristol

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

We got married just over two months ago and our photographer didn't turn up. We're devastated.

We booked her about 18 months before the wedding through Instagram, she runs her business through her page. We paid a £500 deposit when we booked and the remaining £2,000 about eight weeks before the wedding, so £2,500 in total. She did do a pre-wedding shoot for us about two months before the big day which cost an extra £300 (paid separately), and we were happy with those photos, so we had no reason to think anything was wrong.

On the morning of the wedding she just didn't show up. No message, no call, nothing. My mum tried calling her and it rang out. We've sent messages on Instagram, WhatsApp and email since then, nothing. She hasn't even read the messages.

A friend stepped in and took photos on their phone which was kind of them, but it's obviously not the same as professional photography. We want our £2,500 back. We also feel like we should be compensated for the distress, we can never get those moments back.

Her T&Cs mention something about force majeure which I'm worried about, what if she tries to use that as an excuse?
