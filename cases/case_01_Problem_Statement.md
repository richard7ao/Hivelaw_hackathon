# Case 01 — My landlord never protected my deposit

**Tenancy Deposit Case 01** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Fresh

**Claimant:** Jamie Alexander Watson, 28, Birmingham

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

I've been renting a one-bed flat for two years, paying £850 a month. When I moved in I paid a £980.77 deposit to my landlord, he's a private landlord, not an agency. I'm looking to move out now and when I asked about getting my deposit back he got really cagey and wouldn't give me a straight answer.

I googled it and found out that landlords are supposed to register your deposit in a protection scheme within 30 days. I've checked all three schemes, DPS, MyDeposits and TDS, and my deposit isn't in any of them. I also never received any paperwork about where my deposit was being held, which I've read they're supposed to give you.

I just want my deposit back but from what I've read online I think I might be entitled to compensation too. I'm not sure what to do next or how to go about this.
