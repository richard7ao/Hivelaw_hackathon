# Case 03 — The dealer says the car was sold as seen

**Consumer Rights Case 03** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Fresh

**Claimant:** Marcus J. Thompson, 45, Greater London

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

A couple of weeks ago I bought a used BMW 3 Series from a dealership for £14,500. After 12 days the engine management light came on while I was on the motorway and the car went into limp mode, I had to crawl to the hard shoulder and call the AA.

I took it to an independent mechanic who said the turbocharger is failing and it'll cost about £4,320 to fix. I haven't driven the car since.

I contacted the dealership straight away and they basically told me tough luck, said I should have bought the extended warranty and that used cars are 'sold as seen'. I've got the text messages where the salesman actually said that.

I've since read that I might have a right to reject the car under the Consumer Rights Act because it's been less than 30 days, but that window is closing fast. I haven't done anything formal yet because I wasn't sure of my rights. What should I do?
