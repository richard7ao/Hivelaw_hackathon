# Case 04 — The airline says it was extraordinary circumstances

**Flight Delay Case 04** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Fresh

**Claimant:** Priya Sharma, 36

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

In August I had a flight from Heathrow to Rome that was delayed by 7 hours. I've read that I'm entitled to compensation under the flight delay rules, I think it's £220 for the distance.

I submitted a claim on the airline's website the day after I got back and they sent me an automated email saying they'd received it. Three weeks later I got a proper response saying the delay was caused by 'extraordinary circumstances', specifically air traffic control restrictions, and that they therefore weren't liable to pay compensation.

I've been reading about this online and it seems like ATC delays are sometimes a valid defence and sometimes not, depending on the specific circumstances. But I have no way of knowing what actually caused the restriction or whether the airline is just using it as a blanket excuse.

I haven't replied to them yet. I'm not sure whether to accept it, push back, or go to one of those claims management companies. The amount isn't huge but it's the principle, I was stuck in the airport for 7 hours with no proper information. What are my options?
