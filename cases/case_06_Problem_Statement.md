# Case 06 — My old employer won't pay what they owe me

**Employment Wages Case 06** | Lawhive Hackathon 2026 | Case Brief / Messy, Mild

**Claimant:** Ryan Daniel Patel, 27

---

## Problem, in the client's own words

I left my job at a marketing agency five weeks ago. I worked my full four-week notice period and left on good terms, or so I thought.

My final pay was supposed to include my last month's wages (about £2,800 before tax), 8 days of holiday I hadn't taken (roughly £1,050 before tax), and a commission payment of about £1,500 that my manager confirmed in an email before I left.

I've received nothing. Not a payslip, not a penny.

I emailed HR the day after the payment was due and they said 'we're looking into it'. I emailed again two weeks later, no reply. I sent a third email last week saying I'd take further action if I wasn't paid within seven days. I also messaged my old manager directly who said 'nothing I can do, it's with finance'. That deadline was yesterday and still nothing.

I haven't raised a formal grievance or sent any kind of legal letter, I'm not really sure what the process is. I'm also worried about the commission because there's a clause in my contract that says it's 'payable at the company's discretion on active employees'. Does that mean they can just refuse to pay it even though it was confirmed?
