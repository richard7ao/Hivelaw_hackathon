# Judging Criteria — Lawhive Hackathon (Hivelaw)

> The official lens framework the judges score against. **Access to Justice (Lens A)
> is the headline** — every other lens is in service of it. Build decisions in this
> repo should trace back to one or more of these four lenses.

## Lens A — Theme: Access to Justice **(the headline)**

How much could this genuinely unlock access to justice in the UK?

- This is the top-line criterion. **Everything else is in service of it.**
- A build that scores well on B/C/D but doesn't move the needle on access to
  justice is missing the point.
- The wedge to argue: we close the gap between *what a person believes happened*
  and *what a judge will need to see* — the single biggest source of friction at
  the start of any matter.

## Lens B — Agentic First

Something **beyond a chatbot in a UI** — the solution *does things in the real world*.

- Drafting letters is fine; **sending them is better.**
- Reward real-world action over conversation.

## Lens C — User Experience & Product

Not a terminal-demo competition. The judges want a **full, breathing PoC** —
something a real consumer could plausibly use.

- Bonus marks for **multi-modal interaction** or **meeting people where they
  already are** (WhatsApp, voice, email).
- A polished, consumer-grade product surface matters.

## Lens D — Legally Accurate

The output has to feel like **genuine legal advice** — not generic AI waffle
dressed up in legal language.

- Substance over legal-sounding filler.
- Claims should be grounded and defensible.

## How to use this file

When proposing or scoring a feature, state which lens(es) it serves and how. If a
build doesn't clearly advance **Lens A**, question whether it belongs in scope.

| Lens | Headline question | Bonus signals |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Access to Justice** | Does it genuinely unlock access to justice in the UK? | *This is the headline — weight everything toward it.* |
| **B — Agentic First** | Does it *do* things, not just chat? | Sends letters / takes real-world action |
| **C — UX & Product** | Could a real consumer plausibly use this? | Multi-modal; meets people where they are (WhatsApp, voice, email) |
| **D — Legally Accurate** | Does it feel like genuine legal advice? | Grounded, substantive, not waffle |
