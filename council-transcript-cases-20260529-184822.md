## Chairman's Verdict

### Headline

The whole council agrees on the binding constraint, and it isn't "wow" — it's **don't let the agent be legally wrong in front of a room of solicitors.** Every case pick must pass that test first. That demotes the flashiest cases (the dismissal-with-clock, the lost-appeal), because they're the most likely to make the agent say something a Lawhive solicitor contradicts live — which would kill the access-to-justice argument outright.

### Recommendation: Case 7 (escalate, hero) + Case 4 (honest no)

**Hero = Case 7 (council damp & mould).** Chosen against the cautious consensus (which leaned Case 1) for three reasons: (a) it's the domain you actually built — Case 1 means bolting on a second body of law you didn't prepare; (b) it's far stronger on the criterion that wins this panel, *access to justice* (a child's health, not a £980 deposit); (c) **Awaab's Law** turns the "social housing breaks the hook" flaw into a *stronger, more current* hook — a child died from mould in social housing, new statutory deadlines are phasing in now, and the tools to enforce them don't exist. The two objections to Case 7 are both mitigable: **pre-ingest the photo bundles (never parse a folder live on stage)**, and have your **lawyer vet the disrepair reasoning cold** (it's your prepared domain).

**Honest-no = Case 4 (flight delay).** A genuinely defensible "you may not win, and it's £220 — here's the cost/benefit." Light docs (safe live ingestion), the law really is "it depends" (so the no is *correct*, not a dodge), and it needs only an assessment — no drafted letter — so it's cheap to build. Put the emotion in the hero; the integrity in the no.

### The decision rule

- Lawyer confident on disrepair **and** you can pre-ingest the photo bundles reliably → **7 + 4** (recommended).
- Nervous about live document-handling or disrepair scrutiny → fall back to **1 + 4** (safest of all).
- Want the emotional "no" and your lawyer will nail appeal/CPR law → **1 + 10** (the peer review's lean).
- Avoid **Case 9** as the hero unless your lawyer signs off every line — highest correctness risk in the set.

### On scope

Widen the **framing** ("an honest front door that can say no — disrepair first"), not the deep build. Keep the deep build on damp; add only a **shallow assessment path** for the no. Reconcile "judge picks a case" with correctness by letting them pick from a **curated shortlist of 2–3 pre-tested cases**, not all 10. And **retire the Section-21 foil** — no provided case touches it; use "AI confidently gets current law wrong" + Awaab's Law instead.

### The one thing to do first

**Lock the escalate case now, and pre-ingest its real documents into the engine this week** — pre-processed, never parsed live on stage. The demo's credibility lives or dies on the agent handling the actual provided files without faceplanting, and everything downstream (corpus, letter template, hook) depends on which escalate case you commit to.
