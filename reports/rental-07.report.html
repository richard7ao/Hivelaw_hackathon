A specialist housing disrepair solicitor will usually take this on a conditional ('no win, no fee') basis and can recover costs from the Council, so out-of-pocket cost to Crystal should be low; realistically expect several months (the protocol allows the landlord 20 working days to respond to the Letter of Claim, plus time to instruct a single joint expert and for works/negotiation). Act 1 — Your strongest case

Crystal is a secure council tenant with overwhelming evidence that she repeatedly put the Council on notice of damp and black mould (15 emails over eight months, a phone log, photos and a GP letter) and that the problem worsened and recurred after the Council's own remedial works failed. The Council's own inspection report records a Category 1 damp-and-mould hazard and recommended a specialist damp inspection on recurrence that never happened, which supports breach of the landlord's repairing covenant (s.11) and the fitness-for-human-habitation covenant (s.9A), the latter being a continuing duty that bites where mould returns within a month.
